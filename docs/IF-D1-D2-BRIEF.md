# IF-D1 and IF-D2 decision brief

Status: a proposal. This is not a decision, a registration, or a launch. Nothing has been bought, deployed or posted.

HuKiFl1 asks for a yes on two open items from the Inference Finance spec:

- **IF-D1**, official product name. HuKiFl1 proposes; the spec requires a yes before the name is official. The working name today is Inference Finance (short form InferenceFi).
- **IF-D2**, visual brand. HuKiFl1 leads; the spec requires a yes before the visual brand is official. The brand must be original.

Until those yeses, keep the working name and the kit in this repository. A hosted marketing site, a domain and an official X account are separate decisions (IF-D3 and IF-D4). This brief does not ask for them.

## IF-D1: name

These are options for review. None has had a trademark, domain or handle check. Nothing has been registered or bought. This note is not legal advice and is not a purchase.

| Option | Idea | For | Against | This brief |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inference Finance** (working name) | Names the asset and the job, with short form InferenceFi | Plain and accurate for agents and for the people who run them. Neutral between FLOP and Pearl, which are credit sources, not hosts of the AMM. | Generic, and harder to own or to search for. | **Recommended**, at least for the HyperEVM testnet rehearsal. |
| **Creditcurve** | Credits on a stableswap curve, which is also the logo mark | Distinct, tied to the mark, easy to say. | Can read like lending or credit scoring at first glance. | Keep as an alternative. |
| **Workrate** | Every credit has a rate in units of work | Short, and describes the oracle idea. | A common phrase, likely crowded. | Keep as an alternative. |

**Omerswap** was listed earlier in `brand/BRAND.md` as a fourth option (swaps priced in OMER). This brief recommends against it. "Omer" is also the name of a biblical measure and a religious observance, so the word needs a sensitivity review it has not had. The name would also tie the brand to a unit that could change. Do not use Omerswap as the official name.

Recommendation: keep **Inference Finance** for the testnet rehearsal, and decide IF-D1 before any public launch. The logo mark carries no letters, so the visual system can take any of the recommended names later without a redraw.

Suggested domain `inference.finance` is recorded in the spec as an IF-D3 option only. No availability check was run for this brief, and no domain or DNS change is requested.

## IF-D2: visual brand

The kit in this repository is the proposed visual brand, already applied to the marketing-site design and the app screens. Details live in `brand/BRAND.md`, `brand/TYPE_SCALE.md` and `brand/tokens.json`. Screenshots are in `design/`.

- **Mark.** An original SVG curve on a cyan tile, with an ember quote dot. No letters, so it survives a later name change. Wordmark, short wordmark and lockup exist in light, dark, and mono black or white, plus a favicon.
- **Colour.** Ink, cyan and ember on a mist ground, mapped to Arc UI role tokens for light and dark (`--background`, `--surface`, `--accent`, and the rest). Ember is a highlight and a chart series, never a status colour. Contrast is checked to WCAG AA.
- **Type.** Geist, Inter and Geist Mono, SIL OFL, self-hosted. Weights 400 and 500 only. Sentence case, no eyebrow labels, no em dashes.
- **Illustration.** Original hand-written SVG (OMER unit, reward streams, four step glyphs). No third-party art.
- **UI.** Arc UI free core only, from [uiarc.dev](https://uiarc.dev/). Components use tokens, never raw hex. No Arc Pro.
- **Voice.** Exact, plain, and honest about being a testnet rehearsal: unaudited, TEST mocks, nothing deployed, no token exists. FLOP and Pearl are sources of credits; the AMM is a separate protocol on HyperEVM testnet (chain 998).

The art and tokens are original work by HuKiFl1. Code is MIT; brand assets (except fonts, PNGs and launch drafts) are CC BY 4.0, with no trademark rights granted. See `LICENSE` and `LICENSE-ASSETS.md`.

## What this brief is not asking

Please do not treat a yes on IF-D1 or IF-D2 as a yes on anything else:

- No domain purchase and no DNS (IF-D3).
- No official X account (IF-D4). Launch drafts in `content/LAUNCH_DRAFTS.md` stay marked DRAFT, for review, and must not be posted. Posts through @fitchecking go through Marketing.
- No testnet broadcast, no token, no mainnet, and no public launch.
