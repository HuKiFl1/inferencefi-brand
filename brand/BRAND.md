# Inference Finance brand

Inference Finance is a working name. The short form is InferenceFi. Both are used in this kit until naming decision IF-D1 is made.

This is a design kit for a protocol that is not deployed. It is unaudited, and no token exists. Everything here follows from that.

## Name

### Why "Inference Finance"

- **It says what is traded.** The pools hold inference credits, which are prepaid claims on model work. "Inference" names the asset and "Finance" names the job, with no metaphor to explain.
- **It has a working short form.** "InferenceFi" fits a badge, a package scope and a handle.
- **It stays neutral between credit sources.** It does not belong to FLOP or to Pearl, and the protocol is not hosted by either. A name tied to one source would misdescribe it.
- **The risk is genericness.** "Inference" is a common word in AI, so the name is harder to own and to search for. Trademark and domain checks are open (see the README).

### Naming proposal (IF-D1)

These are proposals for review, not decisions. None has had a trademark, domain or handle check, and nothing has been registered or bought.

| Option | Idea | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Inference Finance** (working name) | Names the asset and the job | Plain, and accurate for agents and people | Generic and hard to own |
| **Creditcurve** | Credits on a stableswap curve, which is the logo mark | Distinct, ties to the mark, easy to say | Reads like lending or credit scoring at first glance |
| **Omerswap** | Swaps priced in OMER, the unit of work | Puts the core idea in the name | "Omer" is also the name of a biblical measure and a religious observance, so it needs a sensitivity review. It also ties the brand to a unit that could change. |
| **Workrate** | Every credit has a rate in units of work | Short, and describes the oracle idea | Common phrase, likely crowded |

Recommendation: keep "Inference Finance" for the testnet rehearsal and decide IF-D1 before any public launch. The logo system works with any of the four names, because the mark carries no letters.

## Positioning

**For** agents that buy inference, and the people who run them.

**Inference Finance is** a stableswap AMM design for FLOP and Pearl inference credits.

**It prices every credit in OMER**, one unit of work: 1 OMER = 1 IU = 750k input + 250k output tokens of a tier's reference model. Credits from different providers become comparable, so a swap moves compute for compute instead of guessing a dollar price.

**Unlike** a general AMM, its pools are built for assets that should trade near 1:1 in work. The fee rises when a trade pushes a pool away from the oracle price. Proportional exits always work.

**Where it stands:** a testnet rehearsal on HyperEVM testnet (chain 998) with labelled TEST mocks. Unaudited. Nothing deployed. No token exists.

What the brand must never say:

- that the protocol is live, audited or ready for production;
- that a token exists, has a ticker or a supply, or can be bought;
- anything about price, return or yield;
- that FLOP hosts or runs the AMM. FLOP and Pearl are sources of credits.

## Voice and tone

Write like an engineer explaining a design to a careful reader: exact, plain and unhurried. Say what does not exist yet before someone has to ask.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Lead with what it is: "A stableswap AMM design for inference credits." | Lead with hype: "The future of AI finance." |
| Give a number with its unit: "750k input + 250k output tokens." | Use a vague size: "massive liquidity." |
| Say "design", "rehearsal" and "preview" for things that are not deployed. | Say "launch", "live" or "now available". |
| Name gaps plainly: "There is no on-chain FLOP or Pearl inference-credit token yet." | Hide gaps in a footnote or a tooltip. |
| Write "reward token (TBD)". | Invent a ticker, a supply or a price. |
| Use sentence case for every heading, button and label. | Use Title Case, ALL CAPS or eyebrow labels above headings. |
| Use short sentences and full stops. Use commas or a new sentence instead of a dash. | Use em dashes, exclamation marks or emoji. |
| Use "shares" for LP positions. | Say "LP token". There is none. |
| Say "agent ID". | Say "wallet" when you mean the agent's identity. |

Tone shifts with context. The site explains, the screens instruct, and status copy is short and literal ("Rates stale, swap disabled").

## Logo

The mark is a rounded cyan tile holding a pair of plot axes and a stableswap curve, with one ember dot where a quote sits on the curve. It reads as "a price on a curve" with no letters, so it survives a rename.

All logo art is original and hand-written SVG, generated by `scripts/build-logo.mjs` from the colour roles in `tokens.json`. It uses no font files: the wordmark letters are drawn as monoline strokes.

### Files

All files are in `brand/logo/`.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `mark-light.svg`, `mark-dark.svg` | App icons, avatars, favicons and small spaces |
| `lockup-light.svg`, `lockup-dark.svg` | The default logo: mark plus "Inference Finance" |
| `wordmark-light.svg`, `wordmark-dark.svg` | Text only, where the mark is already on screen |
| `wordmark-short-*.svg` | "InferenceFi", for tight horizontal space |
| `*-mono-black.svg`, `*-mono-white.svg` | One-colour print, photos and embossing |
| `favicon.svg` | Browser tab icon |

Use `light` files on light backgrounds and `dark` files on dark backgrounds. In React, use `shared/brand/Logo.tsx`, which reads the colour tokens and follows the theme.

### Rules

- **Clear space:** keep a margin of at least one quarter of the mark's height on every side. Nothing else goes inside it.
- **Minimum size:** 16px for the mark, 24px tall for the lockup, and 14px tall for a wordmark on its own.
- **Colour:** use only the files above. Don't recolour the tile, swap cyan and ember, or put the mark on a gradient or a photo. On a busy background, use a mono file.
- **Shape:** don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, or redraw the curve. Keep the ember dot on the curve, and never use it as a status light.
- **Lockup:** don't rearrange the mark and the words, or set the name in another typeface beside the mark.
- **Name:** while IF-D1 is open, use "working name" next to the logo the first time it appears on a page.
- **Co-branding:** never place the logo next to FLOP or Pearl marks in a way that suggests a partnership or endorsement.

## Colour

The palette has four colours: ink for text, cyan for the brand and for actions, ember for the quote dot and highlights, and mist for the page. They are mapped onto Arc UI role tokens so that components never use a raw colour. The source of truth is `brand/tokens.json`, which generates `brand/tokens.css`.

| Role token | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--background` | `#F6F8F9` | `#091014` | Page |
| `--surface` | `#FFFFFF` | `#0F181D` | Panels and cards |
| `--surface-raised` | `#FFFFFF` | `#142027` | Menus, popovers |
| `--surface-muted` | `#EEF2F4` | `#1A272E` | Wells and quiet fills |

Rules:

- Cyan is the only accent. Ember is never used for status or for a button.
- Status colours mean status. A trade direction "toward the oracle price" uses the info tone, not success, because it is not a health signal.
- Every text pair listed in `tokens.json` meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for graphics and control boundaries) in both themes. `npm run check:contrast` checks every pair, compositing translucent tints first.

## Type

Geist for display, Inter for body text and Geist Mono for code and figures. All three are open fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted from `@fontsource-variable`. See `brand/TYPE_SCALE.md` for the scale and `brand/fonts/FONTS.md` for the licenses.

## Illustration

Illustrations live in `brand/illustrations/`. They are original, hand-written SVG and they draw only with colour tokens (each with a fallback), so they follow the theme.

- `omer-unit.svg` shows one OMER as a 10 × 4 grid of 25k-token cells: 30 input cells in cyan and 10 output cells in ember.
- `reward-streams.svg` shows three streams (spend, liquidity, fees) feeding a dashed circle for the reward token (TBD).
- `glyph-credit`, `glyph-omer`, `glyph-pool` and `glyph-route` are 48px line glyphs for the how-it-works steps.

Style: 2px to 3px monoline strokes with round caps and joins, flat fills, no gradients inside shapes, no people and no mascots. Charts and grids beat metaphors. No third-party art is used anywhere.
